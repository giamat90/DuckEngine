## Result the owner should know

The spike's 3.3 to 3.8 times speed advantage over `VecDeque` was **not reproduced**: the measured gain for push+pop is 1.3 to 1.6 times (iteration about 6 times). The spike page is corrected. The ring is worth having for its contract (no allocation, failure as a value), not for speed.
