## Result the owner should know

mimalloc is faster in all 14 cases on Windows and 13 of 14 on Linux (medians of three runs); it meets the 2 times criterion on 64 B churn and 8-thread scaling on both systems. It **fails the "nothing slower beyond tolerance" part on Linux** (`Vec` growth, ratio 0.80). Linux ran in WSL2 (a VM), memory use was not measured, two Linux cases are unstable between runs. Nothing is adopted; the decision and ADR-0022 are the owner's.
