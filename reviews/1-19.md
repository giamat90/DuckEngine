## What the change does

Adds `Mat3` and `Mat4` to `base::math`: column-major `repr(C)` data holding columns, `Mat * Vec` and `Mat * Mat`, transpose, determinant, inverse as an `Option`, scale and rotation constructors, and for `Mat4` translation, `from_mat3`, `transform_point3` and `transform_vector3`. Products add left to right, nothing is fused. Pages: `core-runtime.md` (table row, class diagram, decisions of card 1-19), `wiki/README.md` (v0.32).
