## What the change does

- `engine/base/src/simd/scalar.rs`: `F32x4`, the always-built reference (splat, from_array, to_array, `+ - * /`, neg, sqrt, min, max, abs, horizontal_sum with fixed pair order).
- `engine/base/src/simd/sse2.rs`: the same API on SSE2 (x86-64 with `sse2`), bit-identical to the reference; no FMA, no `rcpps`/`rsqrtps`.
- `engine/base/src/simd/mod.rs`: `F32x4` is the target's backend, the scalar reference elsewhere.
- Design note, N-6 spike result (ISA backends inside `base`, chosen by `cfg`) and two UML views in `wiki/architecture/core-runtime.md` section 8.
