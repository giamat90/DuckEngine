## Summary verdict

## What the change does

- `engine/base/src/arena.rs`: `Arena` (fixed capacity, one 64-byte-aligned block allocated by `with_capacity` / `try_with_capacity`, freed in `Drop`, pointer-bump allocation, `reset(&mut self)`), `ArenaBox<'a, T>` (one value), `ArenaVec<'a, T>` (push-only, fixed capacity, derefs to a slice). Failure is a value (`None`, `Err(value)`). Types with drop glue or an alignment above 64 are rejected at compile time. Zero-sized values and empty slices take no space. The closure of `alloc_slice_with` may allocate from the same arena.
- `engine/base/tests/arena_allocations.rs`: with the counting allocator from 1-2, construction is one allocation, allocating, filling and resetting make 0 allocator calls, drop is one deallocation.
- `engine/base/src/lib.rs`, `engine/base/Cargo.toml` (comment: two modules may use `unsafe`).
- Design note and decisions: `wiki/architecture/core-runtime.md` section 8.
- No waiver: `alloc` returns a wrapper type, so `clippy::mut_from_ref` never fires and no `#[expect]` was needed for it.

## TDD evidence

|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | major (design) | block alignment 1 plus padding from the runtime address made fit and `used()` depend on allocator placement (determinism) | fixed: block allocated with a fixed 64-byte `Layout`, padding from the offset; tests added |
