## What the change does

- `gates/architecture.toml`, thread rule: a third alternative `\bthread\s+as\b` makes any rename of a module called `thread` a finding (`use std::thread as th;`, `use self::thread as th;`, `use crate::thread as th;`, `use thread::{self as th};`, a `pub use` re-export renamed later). Plain `use std::thread;` and the harmless items (`sleep`, `current`, `yield_now`, `available_parallelism`, also renamed individually) stay allowed. The reason text says so.
- `tools/arch-check/arch_check.py`: `strip_noncode` also removes the raw-identifier prefix (`r#thread` is `thread`), which closes a hole that affected every `std::`-anchored rule (`std::r#fs`, `std::thread::r#spawn`). Raw strings are still blanked first.
- Tests: class `RealThreadRule` (11 tests) runs the real gate configuration, not a fixture. Wiki: `ports-and-adapters.md` row, README v0.22.
- Not changed: thresholds, workflows, digest, the test-code exemption of card 1-4.
