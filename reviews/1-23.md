## What the change does

Owner decision (2026-10-27): D2 returns for the physics crate only, opt-in (Cargo feature `cross-platform-determinism`, off by default, on in CI), Windows x86-64 and Linux x86-64, ARM64 not gated.

- `gates/architecture.toml`: libm float functions and `mul_add` refused in `physics` (method and path form); fused multiply-add intrinsics refused in `physics` (FMA is used through the facade `madd`, with the feature off, owner request after the first review) and reciprocal-estimate intrinsics refused in `physics` and `base`.
- `tools/arch-check/test_arch_check.py`: the libm fixture is gone (the real config carries the rules); new tests for the libm spellings in `physics`, libm allowed in `base` and `render`, the estimate intrinsics in `physics` and `base`, the fused intrinsics in `physics` (allowed in `base`), look-alike names not flagged, and one documented false positive.
- Wiki: physics section 3 rewritten (D2 level, rules 7 to 10, what each mode promises, a flowchart of the two modes and of the hash comparison), digest section 5, ADR-0007 row, Q9, Q16, R-21, quality metrics, TDD workflow, roadmap, ports-and-adapters, core-runtime, diagrams, competitor survey wording, README v0.36.
