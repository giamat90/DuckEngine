## Decision for the owner to see

The card says "get by index or Handle" and "stale handle detection if keyed by Handle". The set is keyed by a plain `u32` id; the caller passes `handle.index()` and the set does not detect a stale handle. I read the second clause as conditional. A handle-aware wrapper, or `DenseMap<Handle<T>, V>` (which detects stale handles), is the alternative.
